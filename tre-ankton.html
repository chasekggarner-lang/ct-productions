The people behind C&T

Tre Ankton

Client Relations & Project Consultation

A good project starts with understanding people.

Tre leads the client-facing side of C&T Productions. He helps clients explain what they need, understand their options, and shape a clear direction for their website.

His background spans hospitality, retail sales, social media marketing, and running his own business. Across those roles, he has worked directly with customers, answered questions, understood individual needs, and communicated the value of a product or service.

As the owner of Overtime Vending, Tre handled customer inquiries, pursued location partnerships, and managed day-to-day operations. His marketing work for Gleam included creating story-driven short-form video content to introduce an app to new audiences.
