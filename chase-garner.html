The people behind C&T

Chase Garner

Product Design & Development

Turning the idea into something you can use.

Chase leads product design and development at C&T Productions. He builds the websites and digital tools behind each project, translating business needs into clear, usable experiences.

His freelance work includes building and deploying websites for Hibiscus & Beans and Surviving the Hood: The Play. That work has involved collaborating with business owners on design, deployment, domain configuration, and ongoing maintenance.

Chase earned a Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity with a minor in Business from the University at Albany. His research experience includes AI watermarking and a cybersecurity interface designed to make technical risks easier for decision-makers to understand.
