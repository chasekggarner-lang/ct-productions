07 / A little clarity

BEFORE
YOU ASK.

Which package is right for me?

Basic covers a custom, mobile-friendly website and the essentials. Premium adds photography, advanced design, and expanded content. Premium + Chatbot adds automated answers and inquiry assistance. We confirm the fit during consultation.

What do I need to provide?

We gather your business information, logo, and service or menu content. For packages with photography, we coordinate the photo requirements during planning.

Can I review the website before launch?

Yes. Review and revisions are part of the process. You provide feedback before the final version is approved.

What about my domain and ongoing support?

Domain connection is part of the launch process. We discuss ongoing support if needed. Hosting costs, support arrangements, and any recurring fees should be confirmed in your project scope.

How long will my website take?

Timing depends on the agreed scope and when content and photography are ready. We establish a project timeline during consultation.
